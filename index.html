Our craft

Every piece arrives with its story

Aman Bazaar began with a shared love of history and the objects that outlive us. We work with small workshops who still cast, paint and finish by hand, then we photograph, weigh and check each piece ourselves before it is listed.

The result is a bazaar where a candle holder, a collar or a statement table can sit in a modern room and still speak of the civilisation that inspired it.
